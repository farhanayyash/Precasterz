Achievements

Our Milestones & In-House Tools

Beyond client project delivery, Precasterz builds its own engineering automation. Our flagship achievement is the HollowCoreSlab plugin for AutoCAD 2021–2024 — automating precast hollow-core slab layouts, openings, core-fills, shop drawings, and BOQ export from a single source of truth.

HollowCoreSlab Plugin
for AutoCAD 2021–2024

Our in-house AutoCAD plugin automates precast hollow-core slab placement: pick a section, drag a rectangle, and the plugin places full pieces, cut pieces, CIS strips, openings with auto-trim, core-fills with reinforcement, element tags, and exports the BOQ to XLSX — all from one source of truth.
