Services

Precast Structural Design Calculations

We prepare comprehensive precast structural design calculations to ACI 318-19, Eurocode, BS and PCI — covering complete elements and their connections at ultimate and serviceability limit states, with clear, auditable output. Explore our design-calculation services below.

Request a Quote Call +971 50 359 5647

Building Design

Complete precast building design — frame elements, bearing-wall systems and insulated sandwich panels: flexure, shear, connections, deflection and lifting, to ACI 318-19 and PCI.

Learn More

Lifting Design & Analysis

Structural design of precast lifting inserts and handling — anchor capacity, concrete breakout, shear and rigging — to ACI 318-19 and PCI for safe demoulding, handling and erection.

Learn More

Boundary Wall Design

Free-standing precast boundary walls as a complete system: wind load, panel wall and beam action, RC columns, footings, and dowel or pocket connections (including firewall panels), to ACI 318-19, BS 8110 and Eurocode.

Learn More

Firewall Panel Design

Tall free-standing precast firewall panels governed by out-of-plane lateral load (greater of wind and blast overpressure) and stability: flexure and shear, deflection, crack width, base connection and lifting, to BS 8110.

Learn More

Hollow Core Slab Design

Prestressed hollow core slab design across depths from 150 to 500 mm: prestress losses, flexure, shear, deflection and camber, bearing and lifting, to ACI 318-19, PCI and BS EN 1168.

Learn More

Precast Double Tee Slab Design

Long-span prestressed double tee floors and roofs: prestress losses, transfer and service stresses, flexure, shear, camber, vibration, dapped ends and lifting, to ACI 318-19 and the PCI Design Handbook.

Learn More

Prestressed Beam & Culvert Cover Design

Pretensioned precast beams and culvert covers: prestress losses, service stresses, ultimate flexure and shear, anchorage zone, bearing and lifting, to ACI 318-19 and Eurocode.

Learn More

Cover Slab Design

Reinforced precast cover slabs: flexure, shear, crack-width control, standard-hook development length, load cases, bearing and lifting, to ACI 318-19.

Learn More

Footing & Foundation Design

Precast footings and pocket / socket foundations: bearing pressure, flexure, one-way and punching shear, crack width and connection design, to ACI 318-19.

Learn More

Retaining Wall Design

Precast retaining walls: sliding, overturning and bearing-pressure stability, stem and base design, crack-width control and lifting, to ACI 318-19.

Learn More

Block & Mass-Concrete Design

Precast blocks and mass-concrete elements: flexure, shear, crack-width control, interface shear-connection reinforcement and lifting, to Eurocode 2 (EN 1992).

Learn More

Pylon & Signage Design

Precast pylon and signage structures: wind load, footing flexure/shear and punching, crack width, pocket connection and upper-beam design, to ACI 318-19.

Learn More

Yard & Handling-Frame Design

Precast storage-yard and handling-frame design: stacking stresses and yard foundations to ACI 318-19, and steel saddle frames to BS EN.

Learn More
