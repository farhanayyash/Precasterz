Services

Engineering Automation & Quantity Services

Precision meets efficiency. We provide meticulous quantity reporting alongside bespoke automation tools designed to streamline your structural design and documentation workflows.

Request a Quote Call +971 50 359 5647

Bills of Quantities (BOQ) & Bar Bending Schedules (BBS)

We provide detailed quantification and reinforcement scheduling for precast elements, ensuring structural integrity and precise material procurement for every project phase.

Learn more

ETABS Load Combination Automation

Custom-developed scripts that automate the generation of complex load combinations within ETABS, eliminating manual errors and significantly accelerating the structural analysis phase.

Learn more

Concise Beam Autoprinter

An innovative automation tool designed for batch processing and printing of Concise Beam design reports, optimizing your project documentation and reporting workflow.

Learn more

HollowCoreSlab Plugin for AutoCAD

Parametric precast hollow-core slab automation for AutoCAD 2021–2027. Pick a section, drag a rectangle, and place a fully tagged and dimensioned layout — with openings, core-fills, shop drawings, and BOQ export — from one source of truth.

Learn more

Retaining Wall Design Tool

Our in-house design application for a retaining wall with a boundary wall on top, including an adjacent swimming pool: earth, surcharge, wind and pool loads, overturning, sliding and bearing for every load case, factored design forces, and a full PDF calculation sheet in one click.

Learn More

Dapped-End Design Sheet (PCI 8th Edition)

Dapped ends of precast beams and double tees: flexure and axial tension, direct shear, diagonal tension at the re-entrant corner and in the extended end, with bar selection, to the PCI Design Handbook.

Learn More

Column Pocket Foundation Design Sheet

Precast columns in pocket foundations: wall forces from equilibrium with friction, shear friction and flexure of the pocket walls, column shear in the pocket and footing punching, to ACI 318.

Learn More

Steel Saddle Frame Design Sheet

Steel hanger frames supporting hollow core slabs at openings: plate and built-up section design, welds with shear flow, and deflection, to AISC 360.

Learn More
