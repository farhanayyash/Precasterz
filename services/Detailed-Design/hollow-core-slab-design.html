Precast Hollow Core Slab Design

  • Request a Hollow Core Slab Design

We carry out the structural design of prestressed precast hollow core slabs across the full range of depths we produce, typically from 150 mm to 500 mm. Our calculations cover the slab from the moment of strand release through transport, erection and the in-service condition, verifying strength, serviceability and handling for the specified spans and superimposed loads.

The design checks we perform

Prestress force and losses

We establish the jacking and transfer force and calculate short- and long-term losses (elastic shortening, relaxation, shrinkage and creep) to obtain the effective prestress used in every subsequent check.

Stress limits at transfer and in service

We verify concrete fibre stresses against the allowable tension and compression limits at transfer and under service load, so the section stays uncracked where required.

Ultimate flexural capacity

We confirm the factored moment capacity of the prestressed section against the design moment, including strand stress at ultimate.

Shear capacity

We check web-shear and flexure-shear strength to ACI 318-19, allowing for the absence of shear links in standard hollow core sections and the contribution of the prestress.

Deflection and camber

We calculate transfer camber and long-term deflection under sustained and live load, and confirm they are within serviceable limits for the span.

Bearing and end support

We verify bearing length and end-zone stresses at the supports, including any transmission-length effects near the slab ends.

Lifting and handling

We check the clutch / sling arrangement and slab stresses during demoulding, stacking and erection.

Governing standards and how we apply them

ACI 318-19

Primary code for flexural and shear strength, serviceability stress limits and detailing of the prestressed section.

PCI

We follow PCI design practice for prestressed hollow core members, including handling, camber and bearing recommendations.

BS EN 1168 / EN 1992

Used where European practice is specified, with EN 1168 covering the precast hollow core product and EN 1992-1-1 the prestressed concrete design.
