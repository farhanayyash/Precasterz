Retaining Wall Design Tool with Boundary Wall on Top

Our in-house design tool designs a cantilever retaining wall that carries a boundary wall panel above ground, including the case of a swimming pool built next to the wall. Change any input and the section, loads, stability checks and design forces update instantly. One click exports a complete, checkable PDF calculation sheet.

Request a Retaining Wall Design WhatsApp us

Live Section and Load Diagram

Inputs are grouped on the left: footing and upstand, boundary wall panel, wind, retained soil, outer soil and passive resistance, foundation, swimming pool, materials, load factors and acceptance criteria. The section on the right redraws with the earth pressure, pool surcharge, wind and the base reaction for the selected load case, and the status bar shows the governing factors of safety.

Capabilities

Retaining wall with boundary wall on top

Models the full system per metre run: strip footing (with optional toe and shear key), the retaining upstand, and the boundary wall panel above ground with its own height, thickness, unit weight and offset.

Lateral loads

Rankine active earth pressure, ground surcharge, wind on the panel in either or both directions, and optional passive resistance on the outer side with a mobilisation factor. Each surcharge effect can be switched on or off separately for stability, bearing and member design.

Adjacent swimming pool

The lateral pressure from a pool next to the wall is integrated with the Boussinesq strip-load solution (yielding or rigid wall), using the gross or net pool load, for both pool-empty and pool-full cases. Pool weight over the heel is included where it applies.

Stability and bearing

Overturning and sliding factors of safety (minimum 1.5 by default), base friction to ACI 318-19 §22.9.4.2 or from soil friction, bearing pressure with eccentricity and contact-length checks, and pressure redistribution when the resultant leaves the middle third.

Factored design forces

Design moments and shears for the boundary wall panel, the upstand at the top of the footing, and the footing heel and toe at the face of the upstand and at distance d. Load-factor presets for ACI 318 / ASCE 7, BS 8110 and Eurocode, or user-defined factors.

Every load case, every time

Wind outward and inward, pool empty and pool full are all checked automatically. The governing case for each check is reported, so nothing depends on the engineer guessing the critical combination.

Calculation Sheet Summary

The exported PDF starts with a title block and a summary of every check against its limit, with the governing case and an OK/NG status. Input data, figures, load tables, stability, bearing and design forces follow, with an optional detailed section that shows each equation with its substituted values.

Swimming Pool Surcharge

The pool load is applied as a strip load at the pool underside and integrated down the face of the upstand. The figure shows the influence angle and the resulting pressure profile for the pool-empty and pool-full cases.
