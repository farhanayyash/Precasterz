Steel Saddle Frame Design Sheet (Hollow Core Slab Hanger Beam)

Our in-house design sheet for the steel saddle frames that carry hollow core slabs across openings and trimmed areas. From the opening span, slab loads and plate sizes, it designs every plate and weld of the frame to AISC 360 and confirms the deflection limit.

Request a Saddle Frame Design WhatsApp us

Frame Geometry and 3D View

The sheet carries its own plan, sections and plate details, so the dimensions that drive the calculation are visible on the same page.

Input and Loading

Input cells are marked in blue. The sheet works out the line load on the frame from the slab span, floor loads and topping, adds the frame's own weight and gives the factored reaction on each side.

Design checks

Loading

Converts the floor dead and live loads over the slab span into the line load on the frame, with 1.2D + 1.6L for strength and service loads for deflection.

Seat plate

Checks shear yielding and rupture, flexural yielding with the 1.6My limit, and bending stress for the plate carrying the slab ends.

Built-up hanger sections

Calculates the section properties of each built-up section and checks shear strength, the web slenderness limit, flexural yielding and bending stress.

Welds

Checks minimum and maximum fillet weld sizes, the effective throat and weld strength, including the directional strength increase for transverse welds, and the shear flow between flange and web of the built-up sections.

Deflection

Checks the frame deflection under service load against the span/360 limit.

Overall verification

Each section ends with a clear adequate / not adequate result, so the frame can be signed off at a glance.

Section Design Check

Specifications

StandardAISC 360 (14th Edition): chapters F, G and J, with Table J2.4 weld sizes
ElementsSteel saddle frames, hangers and trimmer frames for hollow core slab openings
InputsClear span, width, bearing lengths, loading span, floor loads, slab and topping thickness, plate thickness, Fy, Fu, E
OutputSection-by-section capacity and weld checks with AISC clause references, deflection check and overall verification
FormatProtected Excel workbook with locked formulas and marked input cells
